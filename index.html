Explore amazing holiday experiences with IRCTC

Maharajas' Express

Redefining Royalty, Luxury and Comfort, Maharajas' Express takes you on a sojourn to the era of bygone stately splendour of princely states. Sylvan furnishings, elegant ambience and modern amenities are amalgamated for an Experience Unsurpassed. It has been a winner of World's Leading Luxury Train by World Travel Awards consecutively for the last six years.

International Packages

Best deals in International Holiday packages, handpicked by IRCTC, for Thailand, Dubai, Sri Lanka, Hong Kong, China, Macau, Bhutan, Nepal, U.K., Europe, USA and Australia. Packages include sightseeing, meals, visa charges and overseas medical insurance for a hassle-free experience.

Domestic Air Packages

Be it the spiritual devotee seeking blessings of Tirupati, Shirdi or Mata Vaishno Devi, or the leisure traveller wanting to explore the Blue Mountains of North East, Rajasthan, Ladakh, Himalayas, serene lakes or picturesque islands, IRCTC has it all. Discover India through IRCTC!

Bharat Gaurav Tourist Train

IRCTC operates Bharat Gaurav Tourist Train with AC III-Tier accommodation, specially designed to promote domestic tourism in India. It runs on various theme-based circuits covering pilgrimage and heritage destinations on 5 to 20 days trips, showcasing India's rich cultural heritage.

Rail Tour Packages

IRCTC offers exclusive Rail Tour Packages with confirmed train tickets, sightseeing and meals for destinations like Nilgiri Mountains, Darjeeling, Kullu Manali, Kashmir, Gangtok, Mata Vaishno Devi, Rameswaram, Madurai, Shirdi and Tirupati. Holiday and land packages are also available.
